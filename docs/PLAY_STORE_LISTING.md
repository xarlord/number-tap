## Full Description (max 4000 chars)

🏆 Number Tap — The ultimate speed puzzle challenge!

Race against the clock to find and tap numbers in ascending order on a chaotic grid. Every correct tap buys you time. Every wrong tap costs you. How far can you go?

⚡ HOW TO PLAY
• Numbers are scattered randomly on the grid
• Tap them in ascending order: 1, 2, 3, 4...
• Each correct tap adds time to your countdown
• Wrong taps deduct time — stay focused!
• The grid grows as you level up — 4×4 → 5×5

🎮 GAME FEATURES
• Fast-paced, addictive gameplay — instant state changes, no loading
• Beautiful dark minimalist design — 4 unique themes to unlock
• Combo system — consecutive correct taps earn bonus coins
• Dynamic difficulty — grid grows and time bonuses shrink as you improve
• Daily missions — 3 new challenges every day for extra rewards
• Streak rewards — log in daily to build your streak and earn power-ups
• High score tracking — beat your personal best and share with friends
• Satisfying audio — procedural sound effects that scale with your combo
• Pause & resume — take a break without losing progress

🏆 PROGRESSION
• Score 0–15: 4×4 grid, +1.0s per correct tap
• Score 16–40: 4×4 grid, +0.7s per correct tap
• Score 41+: 5×5 grid, +0.5s per correct tap

💡 POWER-UPS
• Slow Motion — slow the timer for 5 seconds
• Extra Time — add 5 bonus seconds
• Highlight — glow the next target number
• Double Coins — double coin earnings for one game

🎨 THEMES
• Classic Dark — deep slate with white numbers
• Neon Night — vibrant cyan on black
• Ocean Deep — calming blue tones
• Sunset Fire — warm orange and red

Perfect for quick gaming sessions — each game lasts 30–90 seconds. Ideal for commutes, waiting in line, or anytime you need a brain challenge.

Number Tap is completely free to play. Optional rewarded ads give you extra time when you need it most.

Download now and see how fast your brain really is! 🧠
