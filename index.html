🧠 Number Tap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Overview

Number Tap ("the App") is developed by xarlord. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Number Tap does not:

  • Collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Collect device identifiers or location data
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Share any data with third parties
  • Require account registration

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • High score
  • Selected theme preference
  • Sound and music settings
  • Daily login streak
  • Coin balance and power-up inventory
  • Daily mission progress

This data is never transmitted off your device. You can clear it by uninstalling the app or resetting your high score in Settings.

Advertising

Number Tap uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device information for ad personalization. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

For more information on Google's data practices, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Number Tap is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. We do not target children under 13 with advertising.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — Serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. See AdMob Privacy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
